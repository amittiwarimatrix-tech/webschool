Our Story

Building Futures Since 2000

Adarsh Vidyalaya was founded in 2000 with a singular vision — to provide world-class education rooted in Indian values to the children of Rajasthan. What began as a small school with 200 students has grown into one of the most respected educational institutions in the region.

हमारा विद्यालय हर बच्चे के सर्वांगीण विकास के लिए प्रतिबद्ध है। शिक्षा, संस्कार और खेल-कूद का यह अद्भुत संगम यहाँ हर विद्यार्थी को एक उज्जवल भविष्य की ओर ले जाता है।

Today, we are home to over 1,500 students from across Rajasthan, guided by 120+ dedicated faculty members who believe in nurturing every child's unique potential.

Vision, Mission & Values

🔭

Our Vision

To be Rajasthan's most trusted institution that nurtures globally aware, compassionate, and skilled individuals who contribute meaningfully to society.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide holistic education that combines academic rigor with moral values, physical wellness, and emotional intelligence — preparing students for life, not just exams.

💎

Core Values

Integrity, Respect, Excellence, Curiosity, and Service. These five pillars guide every decision we make and every interaction within our school community.
