Eligibility Criteria

Who Can Apply

Class 6 Admission

  • Completed Class 5 from recognized school
  • Age: 10–12 years as of April 1, 2025
  • Minimum 60% in Class 5
  • Entrance test: Hindi, English, Maths

Class 9 Admission

  • Completed Class 8 from recognized school
  • Age: 13–15 years as of April 1, 2025
  • Minimum 65% in Class 8
  • Entrance test: Science, Maths, English

Class 11 Admission

  • Passed Class 10 CBSE/State Board
  • Science stream: Min 75% in Science & Maths
  • Commerce stream: Min 65% overall
  • Interview + document verification

Documents Required

  • Previous school marksheets (last 2 years)
  • Transfer certificate from last school
  • Birth certificate
  • 4 passport-size photographs
  • Aadhar card (student + parents)
